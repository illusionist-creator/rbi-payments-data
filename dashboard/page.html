                                                          Reading the numbers

                                                          Definitions and caveats

                                                          Source
                                                          Reserve Bank of India, Bank-wise ATM/POS/Card Statistics, one Excel release per month. Every figure here is a bank's own monthly return to RBI, re-shaped but not adjusted.
                                                          Payments and cash
                                                          "Card payments" are transactions at PoS terminals plus online/e-commerce and other card-not-present transactions. "ATM cash" is cash withdrawn at ATMs with the card. Before March 2022 RBI reported only an "at PoS" and an "at ATM" split; from March 2022 the payments side is the sum of three published columns. Debit-card cash withdrawn at PoS (tiny) is excluded from both.
                                                          Headline tiles
                                                          Changes are green when they are good news for the market, red when they are bad news (cash advances on credit cards, UPI declines, app concentration) and uncoloured when they are neither (average ticket, cash withdrawn on debit cards). Month-on-month changes on monthly totals and per-card monthly rates compare per-day averages, so a 28-day February is not read as a fall. The line under each tile covers the time window up to the latest month, with the month picked marked; hover or use the arrow keys to read any month. Cards outstanding count every card in issue, used or not; RBI's 2022 card directions make issuers close credit cards unused for a year, and RBI publishes no count of new cards, so spend per card added is net of closures. The concentration tiles mark NPCI's cap: no UPI app above 30% of volume on a rolling three months after 31 December 2026. UPI months marked provisional are the latest three, which NPCI revises.
                                                          Units
                                                          Counts are in Indian notation: K = thousand, L = lakh (100,000), Cr = crore (10 million). Money is in ₹ crore; ₹1 L Cr = ₹1 lakh crore = ₹1 trillion. RBI's own unit changed from ₹ million to ₹ lakh (July 2019) to ₹ thousand (March 2022); all values are converted before display.
                                                          Ticket sizes
                                                          Monthly spend per card = card payments value ÷ cards outstanding. Average payment = payments value ÷ payment count. Average ATM withdrawal = ATM cash value ÷ ATM withdrawal count. All in rupees per transaction or per card per month.
                                                          Market share
                                                          Always a share of all banks in RBI's release for that month, even when a bank-type filter narrows which banks are listed. Colours stay attached to a bank while you change the window, month or Top N.
                                                          Bank names
                                                          RBI has spelt the same bank many different ways since 2011. Names are standardised to RBI's latest spelling, so mergers show as the surviving bank from the merger month and the absorbed banks simply end. Bank type follows RBI's own group headings, with IDBI Bank classed as private from its 2019 reclassification onward.
                                                          Revisions
                                                          RBI re-issues some months as "Revised", sometimes a year later. The data file is rebuilt from the latest release of every month whenever the updater runs.
                                                          UPI
                                                          The UPI section comes from NPCI's UPI Ecosystem Statistics tables, not from RBI. App shares use customer-initiated transactions as NPCI reports them per app; app names are standardised across NPCI's spelling changes. Remitter-bank shares come from NPCI's top-50 member table, so "Others" there is everything outside the top 50. NPCI revises the latest months, and the collector re-reads the last three months on every run. The state map uses NPCI's state-wise table (from April 2023); a large share of transactions carries no location and is left off the map, so state shares are of located transactions only. Boundaries: udit-001/india-maps-data, simplified.
                                                          Insights
                                                          The Insights view reads RBI's card figures and NPCI's UPI tables together, so its month picker starts in April 2020 when NPCI's monthly data begins; panels that use RBI data alone follow the full time window. Every finding is a rule applied to the numbers for the month picked (for example, a finding about new cards appears only when spend per added card is below 80% of the average), so findings appear, disappear and change column as the data changes. "Spend per card added" is the 12-month change in card spend divided by the 12-month change in cards outstanding. UPI decline rates come from NPCI's remitter-bank table (top banks by volume, weighted by volume): "customer-side" declines (NPCI calls them business declines) are refusals for insufficient balance, wrong PIN or exceeded limits, and say more about a bank's customer base than about how well it runs; "technical" declines are system failures. Chargebacks are NPCI's beneficiary-bank chargeback table summed across banks. Per-person figures use approximate 2026 population projections from the National Commission on Population and count only transactions NPCI could place in a state. Seasonality is the average deviation of a month from a centred 13-month trend over the five years ending six months before the month picked.
                                                          Risk monitor
                                                          The Risk monitor rates card issuers against their peers, separately for credit and debit cards. Peers are issuers with at least a lakh credit cards, or at least 10 lakh debit cards, in the month picked (largest 25, within the bank-type filter). Cells are shaded by quartile among those peers on indicators where a direction is worse. Growth rates are withheld for issuers whose card base a year earlier was under a fifth of today's. Flags common to both: New cards under-used (cards grew over 5%, and spend or payments per added card is under 60% of the issuer's average and no better than the market's ratio; the ratio is computed only when the card base grew at least 2%); Spend / Payments lagging issuance (value growth trails card growth by 5 points more than it does for the market as a whole); Low card activity (worst quartile of transactions per card); Losing spend / payments share (down more than 0.5 points in a year); Portfolio purge (cards outstanding fell over 5% in a single month within the last 12); High UPI decline rate and High chargebacks (worst quartile among peers with NPCI data); UPI technical declines (over 0.5% of attempts); Systemic weight (over 20% of all cards of that type). Credit only: High cash-advance use (worst quartile of ATM withdrawals per 1,000 cards) and Cash advances rising (over 20% in a year). Debit only: Cash-dependent base (worst quartile of ATM cash withdrawn per card) and ATM network shrinking (the bank's own ATMs down over 5% in a year, counted only for banks with at least 50 ATMs). UPI indicators describe the bank as a UPI remitter or beneficiary, not its card book, and exist only for the banks NPCI lists. Views can be linked directly: add #risk-credit, #risk-debit or #insights to the address.
